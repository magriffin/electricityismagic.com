EIM011

Eric Powell
Ballads of Steel and Linoleum

We should do this again on a cool, crisp day
A Low and Distant Roar
(William) Two for Two Twenty-Four.
Steve
Across the Water

This album has been in the works since 2007, when I first started talking with my soon-to-be EiM co-Artistic Director Matt Griffin about a piece for trombone and airplanes. Since then, I have been writing music for odd combinations of instruments and environmental sounds. Many thanks to the EiM crew for always pushing the edges of contemporary music, and for helping make this album a reality.

Ballads of Steel and Linoleum features
Matthew Griffin: Trombone
Nigel Taylor: Trumpet
Eric Powell: Vibraphone, Mandolin, Autoharp.

All tracks (except "Steve") recorded in the War Room (Regina and Montreal) between 2010 and 2014.
"Steve" was recorded live at "Holophon at the Artesian," May 25, 2011.

All tracks composed, recorded, mixed, and mastered by Eric Powell.

Eric Powell

Recorded live on Swede Lake, a small body of water deep in the woods of Northern Saskatchewan. This project explored the natural acoustics of this small lake, playing with the echo and and natural filtration effects discovered on site.
